Scholarships for Indian Students 2026-2027

If you have completed your Class 12 board exams or a bachelor's degree from an Indian university, where do you begin looking for funding to study in the United States, the United Kingdom, Germany, Canada or Australia? The Ministry of Education's National Scholarship Portal is the first place many Indian students check, though most overseas funding comes from the destination country or the university itself. Knowing which route fits your level, whether undergraduate, postgraduate or research, shapes everything that follows.

Several international scholarships are open to Indian applicants and cover tuition, living costs or both, making them effectively fully funded. The Fulbright-Nehru programme for master's and doctoral study in the US, Chevening for one-year UK master's degrees, DAAD for Germany, Erasmus Mundus joint master's across Europe, and the Chinese Government Scholarship are among the established routes. Each has its own eligibility rules, typically tied to your degree, work experience and English test scores such as IELTS or TOEFL, and some, like the Commonwealth and Inlaks awards, are aimed specifically at Indian candidates.

Applications usually run through the scholarship body's own portal, and for Fulbright-Nehru and several others you apply via the United States-India Educational Foundation rather than directly. Check whether you need to secure an admission offer first, since Chevening and DAAD expect it, while Fulbright-Nehru does not. Keep your transcripts, degree certificates and test scores ready well before the deadline, as Indian universities can take time to issue duplicates.

Frequently asked questions

Can I apply for a Chevening Scholarship while I'm still working in India?

Yes, Chevening is open to Indian applicants who have work experience and intend to return home after their UK master's. You apply directly through the Chevening portal, and your Indian employer does not need to sponsor you. The main requirement is meeting the programme's work-experience and English-language criteria.

Do I need to take IELTS or TOEFL for a DAAD scholarship to study in Germany?

It depends on the course language and the specific DAAD programme, since some require German proficiency while English-taught courses ask for IELTS, TOEFL or an equivalent. Many German universities also accept a Medium of Instruction certificate from an Indian institution, but not all do. Check the individual programme's language requirement before applying.

Is the Fulbright-Nehru Master's Fellowship only for certain fields, or can students from any Indian university apply?

The Fulbright-Nehru Master's Fellowships are open to applicants from across India, though they prioritise specific fields such as public health, environmental studies, and certain social sciences. Graduates from any recognised Indian university can apply provided they meet the eligibility conditions. Selection is based on academic merit, leadership potential, and the fit of your proposed study plan.

Can I apply for a Chinese Government Scholarship (CSC) through an Indian university, or must I apply directly?

Indian students can apply for the CSC either through the Chinese Embassy in India or directly to a designated Chinese university, depending on the specific category. Some Indian universities also nominate candidates under bilateral exchange arrangements. Applying through the embassy route usually requires submitting your documents within the embassy's stated window.

Do HEC Pakistan or other country-specific scholarships accept Indian students?

Most country-specific scholarships, including those run by HEC Pakistan, are meant for that country's own citizens, so Indian students generally cannot apply. For India-based students, the relevant options are programmes like Fulbright-Nehru, Chevening, DAAD, and the Commonwealth Scholarships, along with Indian government schemes such as the National Overseas Scholarship. Always confirm citizenship eligibility before preparing an application.
